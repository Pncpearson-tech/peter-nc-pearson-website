About the book

Understanding the British is a short, illustrated guide to the history of the United Kingdom, its people and the English language.

From the Anglo-Saxons and Vikings to the British Empire and the modern United Kingdom, it explores the events and ideas that have shaped Britain over the centuries.

Written especially for adult learners of English, but also for anyone curious about British history and culture, the book combines clear explanations with maps, illustrations and occasional touches of humour.

Each of its twelve chapters includes quiz and discussion questions, making it suitable for individual reading, classroom use or conversation groups.

About the author

Peter N C Pearson is a writer and teacher of English to speakers of other languages (ESOL), based in Somerset, England.

His work with Ukrainian refugees, helping them improve their English and settle into life in Britain, led to many conversations about British history, culture and the sometimes puzzling habits of the British people.

He realised that understanding the language was only part of the challenge. Understanding how Britain became the country it is today could be just as helpful — and often just as interesting.

Understanding the British grew out of those conversations, and from Peter’s own enthusiasm for history and the English language. He hopes the book will be enjoyed not only by learners of English, but by anyone curious about the story of Britain.

Peter is often accompanied by his dog, Dottie, who makes occasional appearances throughout the book and even manages to share the front cover with King Athelstan.
