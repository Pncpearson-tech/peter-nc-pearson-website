Why did a king burn the cakes? Who was the first king of all England? And how did English become the language we speak today?

Explore the bookAbout the book

Is it possible to understand the British?

It's not easy! But a little history can help explain a great deal about the country, its people and its language.

Understanding the British takes readers on a lively illustrated journey through the history of the United Kingdom. Written with adult learners of English in mind, it also welcomes anyone curious about how Britain came to be what it is today.

Across twelve chapters, maps and illustrations help tell the story, while questions for quizzes and discussion invite readers to think further.

About the author

Peter N C Pearson is a writer and teacher of English to speakers of other languages (ESOL). His conversations with learners about British history, culture and everyday life helped inspire Understanding the British.

His dog Dottie makes occasional appearances throughout the book — including on the cover, where she is keeping Athelstan company.
